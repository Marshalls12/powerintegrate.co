The Engagement

Three steps.
Permanent results.

Every project follows the same proven sequence — audit, build, automate. In that order. Every time.

Step 01 — Audit
We map where your data lives and why it's broken.
A structured discovery session surfaces all your data sources, manual bottlenecks, and reporting gaps. You walk away with a clear architecture map before we write a single query.
Step 02 — Build
The Data Chassis goes in. Reports come out.
We build your semantic model, Power Query pipelines, and Power BI dashboards — structured for your team to own, not just use. Every measure documented. Every source named.
Step 03 — Automate
The Monday report runs itself. Every day.
Power Automate flows trigger on schedule or on event. Sources refresh. The dashboard updates. Your team opens it and it's already done — no one touched it, nothing broke.
